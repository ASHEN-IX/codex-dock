# Community conduct

Contributors and users should be able to participate respectfully, regardless
of experience, background, identity, disability, or preferred tools.

Discuss ideas and code directly. Explain disagreements, listen to corrections,
and give actionable feedback. Harassment, threats, discriminatory insults,
deliberate disruption, sexual harassment, and publishing private information
without consent are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when
needed. They should consider the context, communicate the reason when possible,
and apply these expectations consistently. Good-faith reports and technical
disagreement must not be punished.

For sensitive conduct concerns, use a maintainer's published private contact.
If none is available, request a private reporting channel without publishing
personal information. Do not use a public issue to expose another person.
